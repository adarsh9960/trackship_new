The Power of Kirana Store Integration

In India's rapidly evolving commercial landscape, neighborhood Kirana stores represent the absolute cornerstone of hyper-local commerce. With over 12 million Kirana outlets spread across metropolitan cities, Tier-2 towns, and rural hubs, these stores possess unmatched proximity to consumers, existing customer trust, and continuous daily footfall.

TrackShip courier & logistics LLP is revolutionizing last-mile delivery by integrating full-fledged courier booking, parcel pick-up & drop-off (PUDO), e-commerce return processing, and micro-distribution capabilities directly into existing Kirana retail stores. Through our proprietary Kirana Partner Platform, shop owners can leverage just 50 to 100 square feet of counter space to generate a lucrative secondary revenue stream without disturbing their daily grocery transactions.

By joining hands with TrackShip, Kirana store partners tap into India's booming e-commerce supply chain, serving as essential neighborhood hubs for major national e-commerce portals, corporate logistics shippers, and local retail customers.

How It Works

The 4-Step Kirana Logistics Workflow

Operating a TrackShip Kirana point is designed to be seamless, fast, and fully automated so you can focus on customer service.

1

Customer Walks In

Retail customers or e-commerce buyers bring parcels for dispatch, pick-up, or return directly to your Kirana store counter.

2

Quick POS Scan

Scan the parcel barcode using the TrackShip app or Bluetooth scanner, weigh the package, print a shipping label, and accept payment.

3

Secure Staging & Handover

Store booked parcels on dedicated shelves. TrackShip express pickup riders collect staged parcels twice daily for hub distribution.

4

Automated Daily Payouts

Commissions, handling fees, and retail booking margins credit automatically to your digital wallet ledger with direct bank transfers.

Simple Setup Prerequisites

Requirements & Store Specifications

TrackShip has streamlined the Kirana Model Franchise to ensure minimal operational friction. You don't need expensive machinery or complex infrastructure—just basic retail utilities.

  • Physical Space: Minimum 50 to 100 sq ft dedicated counter and shelf space inside or adjacent to your existing retail store for parcel staging.
  • Hardware & Gadgets: Android Smartphone (Android 9.0 or above) with active internet, Bluetooth thermal label printer, digital weighing scale (up to 30kg capacity), and Bluetooth barcode scanner (provided in starter kit).
  • Store Location: Ground floor retail store located in a residential neighborhood, main market area, commercial hub, or high-density housing complex.
  • Documentation Required: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Shop & Establishment License / FSSAI License, Bank Account details / cancelled cheque, and Store Electricity Bill.

Got Questions?

Kirana Franchise FAQs

Everything you need to know about starting and running a TrackShip Kirana Franchise.

What is a TrackShip Kirana Model Franchise?

The TrackShip Kirana Model Franchise is a hyper-local logistics partnership designed specifically for retail store owners, Kirana shop proprietors, and small business operators. It enables you to integrate full courier booking, parcel pick-up and drop-off (PUDO), e-commerce return collection, and last-mile delivery services directly into your existing retail shop using just 50 to 100 sq ft of space.

Do I need to stop or change my current grocery business?

Not at all! The Kirana franchise model runs seamlessly alongside your existing grocery, provision, stationery, or retail business. In fact, it complements your store by bringing in new footfall every single day.

How much physical space is required in my shop?

You only need 50 to 100 sq ft of space inside or adjacent to your counter. This space is used to place a small counter setup for weighing/scanning and a secure shelf for staging incoming and outgoing packages.

How much can a Kirana partner expect to earn monthly?

Average Kirana franchise partners earn between ₹35,000 to ₹65,000+ per month through a combination of high retail courier booking margins (up to 45%), per-package PUDO fees, return processing commissions, hyper-local express delivery payouts, and indirect grocery sales growth.

How are booking payments and cash on delivery (COD) settled?

All earnings and commissions are logged in real-time inside your TrackShip Kirana Partner mobile app. COD amounts collected from customers are deposited into your pre-funded digital wallet, and net earnings are automatically transferred directly to your registered bank account on a daily or weekly basis.

What equipment or hardware is provided by TrackShip?

TrackShip provides a complete starter hardware kit which includes a Bluetooth barcode scanner, a high-speed thermal label printer, a digital weighing scale, official illuminated store signboards, marketing flyers, and tamper-evident courier packaging materials.

How do parcels get collected from my Kirana store?

TrackShip operates dedicated hub pickup vehicles and route riders. A designated TrackShip logistics rider visits your Kirana store twice daily (morning and evening) to scan and collect all staged outgoing parcels and drop off incoming parcels for local customer pickup.

Is territorial exclusivity provided for my Kirana store?

Yes! TrackShip enforces a strict territorial protection policy, ensuring that no other TrackShip Kirana Franchise point is authorized within your immediate operating pincode radius (typically 1.5 km to 2 km depending on city density).

What is the process and timeline to start operations?

The entire onboarding process takes just 48 to 72 hours. Once you submit your application and complete document verification, your hardware kit and branding materials are dispatched, store owner training is completed online or in-person, and your store goes live!
