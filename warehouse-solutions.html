Warehousing Capabilities

Strategic Locations

Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune, Ahmedabad. Near highways, ports, airports.

Flexible Storage

Pallet, bin, shelf, bulk, temperature-controlled (2-8°C, 15-25°C), hazmat certified, high-value cage storage.

Value-Added Services

Kitting, bundling, labeling, repacking, quality inspection, FIFO/FEFO, lot/batch tracking, serial number capture.
