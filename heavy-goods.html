Specialized Heavy Goods Transport

Moving large machinery, industrial equipment, or bulk raw materials requires specialized expertise. TrackShip courier & logistics LLP offers end-to-end project cargo solutions designed for the heavyweights of industry.

Our dedicated fleet and heavy-lift capabilities ensure that even the most challenging loads reach their destination safely and on schedule.

  • Flatbed & Specialized Trailer Fleet
  • Full Truckload (FTL) & Part Truckload (PTL)
  • Route Planning & Permit Management
Specialized Equipment & Expertise

Heavy Goods Transport Capabilities

Specialized Fleet

Low-bed trailers (24-48 tons), hydraulic axles, extendable trailers, crane trucks. Capacity up to 150 tons per shipment.

Route Engineering

Route surveys, bridge analysis, turning radius calculation, utility clearance. Permits from RTO, NHAI, state authorities.

Industry Verticals

Wind energy components, transformers, construction machinery, plant relocation, ODC cargo, project logistics.

Safety Standards

IMO/ILO lashing standards, certified riggers, real-time condition monitoring, comprehensive transit insurance.

Project Management

Dedicated project manager, milestone tracking, stakeholder coordination, documentation, post-delivery audit.

Pan-India Network

Operational in all major industrial corridors. Warehousing at key junctions. 24/7 control room monitoring.

TrackShip courier & logistics LLP is a premier courier and logistics provider offering end-to-end supply chain solutions across India.
