Resilient Supply Chain Solutions

TrackShip courier & logistics LLP helps enterprises build agile and resilient supply chains. We go beyond simple transportation to provide strategic insights that drive cost savings and operational excellence.

End-to-End Optimization

Supply Chain Management Services

Network Design

Facility location optimization, multi-echelon inventory, transport mode selection, scenario modeling, TCO analysis.

Inventory Optimization

Demand forecasting (ML), safety stock calculation, ABC/XYZ classification, VMI, consignment stock, dead stock reduction.

Transport Management

Carrier selection, rate benchmarking, route optimization, load consolidation, freight audit, claims management.

Digital Twin & AI

Supply chain digital twin, predictive analytics, disruption alerts, autonomous planning, carbon footprint tracking.

Vendor Management

Supplier onboarding, performance scorecards, risk assessment, strategic sourcing, contract negotiation support.

Sustainability

Green logistics, modal shift (road→rail), EV fleet, packaging optimization, circular supply chain, ESG reporting.
