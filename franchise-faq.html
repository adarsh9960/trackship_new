Franchise FAQs

Home Franchise FAQs

What is the minimum investment for a Trackship franchise?

Our retail courier point model starts at just ₹19,999. Logistics Hubs and Regional Master models require higher investment based on city tier and capacity.

How long does the onboarding process take?

Typically, you can go live within 7-14 days. This includes portal setup, documentation, and the initial training phase.

Do I need previous experience in logistics?

While experience is a plus, it is not mandatory. Our comprehensive training and dedicated support managers guide you through every operational detail.

What kind of space do I need?

For a Courier Point, a shop or office space of 150-200 sq ft is sufficient. Logistics Hubs require at least 1,000 sq ft of warehouse-capable space.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total investment?

Courier Point: ₹19,999 | Logistics Hub: ₹4,99,999 | Regional Master: ₹19,99,999. Includes branding, training, technology setup, and initial marketing kit.

What is the ROI timeline?

Courier Point: 6-8 months | Logistics Hub: 12-15 months | Regional Master: 18-24 months. Based on average partner performance in Tier-1 cities.

Do I need prior logistics experience?

No. Our 7-day training covers operations, technology, sales, and customer service. We prefer entrepreneurial mindset over industry experience.

What territory do I get?

Exclusive pin-code based territory. Courier Point: 3-5 pin codes | Logistics Hub: 15-20 pin codes | Regional Master: District/State level.

How are leads generated?

Corporate marketing drives national leads. Local digital campaigns. Corporate accounts allocated. Walk-in customers from brand visibility.

What ongoing fees apply?

Royalty: 8-12% of revenue. Technology fee: ₹2,999/month. Marketing contribution: 2%. No hidden fees. Transparent monthly statements.
