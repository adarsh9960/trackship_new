Our Story

Founded in 2019, Built on Trust

Trackship Logistics started with a simple vision: to make logistics seamless and accessible for every business in India. What began as a small courier operation has grown into a pan-India network trusted by thousands of clients.

Our growth is a testament to our commitment to excellence, innovation, and customer satisfaction. We continue to invest in technology and infrastructure to stay ahead of the curve.

Our Journey

Timeline of Growth

2019

The Beginning

Trackship Logistics was founded in Mumbai with a small team and a big dream to redefine Indian logistics.

2020

Digital Expansion

Launched our first tech-enabled tracking system and expanded services across 5 major states.

2021

Franchise Launch

Introduced our unique franchise model, empowering entrepreneurs to start their own courier business.

2022

B2B Milestone

Partnered with 200+ direct B2B clients and expanded our footprint to 500+ cities nationwide.

2023

National Scale

Achieved 750+ city coverage across 28 states. Launched green energy initiatives with EV fleet pilots in major metros.

2024

Tech-First Logistics

Deployed AI-powered route optimization and predictive analytics. Launched customer dashboard with real-time visibility.

2025

Ecosystem Expansion

Crossed 1,000 franchise partners. Integrated with ONDC and major e-commerce platforms for seamless order fulfillment.

2026

Sustainable Future

Committed to carbon-neutral operations by 2030. Solar-powered hubs, 30% EV fleet, and zero-waste packaging initiative live.
