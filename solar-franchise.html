The Future of Net-Zero Logistics in India

As India commits to reaching Net-Zero carbon emissions by 2070 and rapid ESG compliance mandates take hold across global e-commerce and corporate supply chains, traditional fossil-fuel dependent logistics centers are facing skyrocketing fuel costs and strict carbon regulations. Green logistics is no longer a future concept—it is the single highest-growth commercial sector in modern supply chain management.

TrackShip courier & logistics LLP is proud to launch India's first dedicated Solar-Powered Clean Logistics Franchise. Our solar hubs combine rooftop photovoltaic (PV) solar micro-grids, lithium-battery energy storage systems (BESS), smart EV fleet charging docks, and solar-powered cold chain storage under one unified operational roof.

By operating a TrackShip Solar Franchise, partners dramatically reduce monthly electricity and fuel operational expenses (OPEX) by up to 75%, while securing lucrative green-certified delivery contracts from top multinational e-commerce platforms, pharmaceutical leaders, and corporate enterprises.

Technical Excellence

Site & Electrical Requirements

TrackShip's engineering team handles turnkey solar installation, net-metering approvals, and EV charger setup. To ensure rapid deployment, candidate sites should meet basic physical criteria.

  • Shadow-Free Rooftop Area: Minimum 300 sq ft to 1,500 sq ft unshaded rooftop or open shed space with southern structural orientation for maximum solar irradiance.
  • Grid Connection: 3-Phase electrical connection with sanctioned load clearance for net-metering synchronization and EV charger installation.
  • Location Accessibility: Ground floor warehouse or commercial shop with easy driveway access for EV 3-wheeler cargo vans and delivery riders.
  • Documentation Required: Property ownership documents / lease agreement, GST registration, Electricity Bill, PAN & Aadhaar of proprietor.

Solar Franchise FAQs

Everything you need to know about setting up and running a TrackShip Solar Powered Franchise.

What is a TrackShip Solar Powered Logistics Franchise?

A TrackShip Solar Powered Logistics Franchise is a state-of-the-art clean energy micro-warehouse that integrates rooftop solar photovoltaic (PV) generation, electric vehicle (EV) charging docks, solar-powered cold storage, and green delivery operations under one unified business model.

How much rooftop space is required for solar panel installation?

Space requirements vary by franchise tier: Tier 1 (Solar Express Point) requires ~300 sq ft of unshaded roof, Tier 2 (Solar Distribution Hub) requires ~1,000 sq ft, and Tier 3 (Master Hub) requires 2,000+ sq ft of rooftop or open shed space.

What happens on rainy or monsoon days when solar output drops?

TrackShip Solar Hubs operate as hybrid grid-connected systems equipped with Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) battery storage. On low-sunlight days, the system automatically draws seamless backup power from the electrical grid or battery bank without interrupting operations.

Are government subsidies and tax incentives available?

Yes! Solar franchise hub owners qualify for up to 40% Accelerated Depreciation IT tax benefits under Income Tax Section 32, central PM-KUSUM / CFA solar subsidies, and state-level EV infrastructure subsidies. TrackShip assists with all government filings.

Can I charge public commercial electric vehicles at my hub for income?

Absolutely! TrackShip EV charging docks feature dual-tariff metering. You can utilize free solar energy to charge your own logistics fleet while offering commercial fast-charging services to public EV auto-rickshaws, bikes, and delivery riders for direct cash income.

What is the lifespan and warranty of the solar panels and battery storage?

All Tier-1 Monocrystalline solar panels come with a 25-year performance warranty. Inverters and LiFePO4 battery storage systems carry comprehensive 5-year to 10-year manufacturer warranties backed by TrackShip's nationwide maintenance team.

Why do enterprise e-commerce platforms prefer shipping through Solar Hubs?

Top national e-commerce brands have strict corporate ESG (Environmental, Social & Governance) mandates to reduce scope 1 and scope 3 carbon emissions. Shipping through TrackShip's verified green solar hubs provides them with official carbon offset credentials.

What is the estimated break-even period and ROI?

Due to the dual advantage of zero vehicle fuel costs, drastic electricity savings, higher parcel margins, and EV charging income, most Solar Franchise partners achieve full capital payback within 10 to 14 months of operation.
